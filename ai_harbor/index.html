The tide table

The simple rule

AI moves too quickly for passive browsing. Work in tides — daily, weekly, monthly — and keep your attention for what matters.

🌅

Daily

Scan headlines and paper feeds. Ten minutes, no more.

🌊

Weekly

Read one curated newsletter and one official lab update.

🌙

Monthly

Revisit your source list and cut low-signal channels.

⚓

For decisions

Charting a course

A practical routine

A layered routine keeps you informed while protecting your attention. Four steps, repeated.

1

Scan

Spend 10 minutes with TechCrunch AI, VentureBeat AI, Hugging Face Papers, or arXiv abstracts.

2

Select

Save only items that affect your tools, projects, industry, or research interests.

3

Deep read

Open the original lab post, paper, docs, or technical report behind each important headline.

4

Apply

Translate each update into an action: test a tool, update a workflow, or ignore it.

How to evaluate AI information

Coverage mixes product marketing, research claims, policy speculation, and investment narratives. Classify the source before you trust the claim.

🏛️

Official lab posts

Best for what a company says it shipped, changed, or believes.

🔬

Research feeds

Best for technical novelty — but stay skeptical about evaluation quality.

📰

News publications

Best for market, startup, legal, and enterprise context.

✉️

Curated newsletters

Best for reducing volume and finding what deserves a deeper read.
